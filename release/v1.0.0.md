## Sari-Sari Store v1.0.0

First public release of the offline-first sari-sari inventory and business app.

### Install on Android

1. Download **sari-sari-store-1.0.0.apk** below
2. Transfer to your phone if needed
3. Open the APK and allow **Install from unknown sources**
4. Launch **Sari-Sari Store**

### Included features

- Dashboard with store health score
- Inventory management with photos
- Fast sales & checkout
- Restocking & stock history
- Low-stock & expiration alerts
- Expenses & profit reports
- PIN / biometric lock
- Backup & restore

### Requirements

- Android device (offline-first; no internet required for daily use)
